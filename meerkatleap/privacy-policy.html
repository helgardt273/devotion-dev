Meerkat Leap privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Meerkat Leap ("the app") is a free game by Devotion Dev. This policy explains what data is handled and why.

No account, data stays on your device

The app has no sign-up or account. Your game progress (coins, best distance, unlocked hats and scarves, upgrades, missions, daily-gift streak) and your settings (theme, vibration) are stored only on your device. We do not receive or store this data on any server. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. If you use Android's own device backup, Google may back it up as part of your phone backup under your Google account.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob to stay free. AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device information and ad interactions in order to show, measure and protect ads. See Google's policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › "Privacy & ad choices". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Ads are never shown while you are running. Optional reward videos are only shown when you tap a button that asks for one.

No purchases, no other tracking

There are no real-money purchases. The app does not use analytics of its own, does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files, and itself declares only the internet and network-state permissions, for ads.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Other permissions and Google Play services

Google's ads SDK and Android libraries also add the advertising-ID, Android ad-services (Privacy Sandbox) and background-work permissions (such as wake lock and foreground service). The app does not show a prompt for these. The "Privacy & ad choices" button appears only where consent applies. The app may also show Google Play's in-app review prompt, which Google Play handles under its own terms.
