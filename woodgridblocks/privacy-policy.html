Woodgrid Blocks: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Woodgrid Blocks ("the app") is a block puzzle game published by Devotion Dev. This policy explains what information the app handles.

No account, data stays on your device

The app has no sign-up or account and no servers of its own. The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • your settings (theme, sound, vibration),
  • your best scores, number of games finished and Daily Puzzle streak,
  • the game you are currently playing, so you can resume it.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of this. If you use Android's own device backup, the system may include this data in your personal Google backup.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (and approximate location derived from it), app interaction and diagnostic data, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. This processing is done by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent (UMP)

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy & ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Permissions

The app itself declares only the Internet and network-state permissions, which are needed to load ads. The game itself works fully offline.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.

Other permissions and Google Play services

Google's ads SDK and Android libraries also add the advertising-ID, Android ad-services (Privacy Sandbox) and background-work permissions (such as wake lock and foreground service). The app does not show a prompt for these. The "Privacy & ad choices" button appears only where consent applies. The app may also show Google Play's in-app review prompt, which Google Play handles under its own terms.
