Patience Parlour privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Patience Parlour is a solitaire card game app (Klondike, Spider and FreeCell) published by Devotion Dev. This policy explains what information the app uses.

No account, no personal information

You do not need an account or sign-up. The app does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

What is stored on your device

The app stores your settings (theme, table colour, card options), your games in progress (including undo history), and your statistics (games played and won, streaks, best times and scores, Daily Deal streaks). This data stays on your device in the app's private storage. We do not receive it. It may be included in your own Android device backup if you have backups switched on. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, a Google service. Ads appear on the game menu and the win screen, never on the card table during play. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), and information about your device and app use. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings.

Consent (UMP)

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app shows Google's consent form (the User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy & ad choices in the app.

Optional rewarded videos

You can choose to watch a short video to get extra hints or to switch off ads for 24 hours. These are always optional.

Permissions

The app itself declares only the internet and network-state permissions, which the ad SDK needs. All game features work offline.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Families programme.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Other permissions and Google Play services

Google's ads SDK and Android libraries also add the advertising-ID, Android ad-services (Privacy Sandbox) and background-work permissions (such as wake lock and foreground service). The app does not show a prompt for these. The "Privacy & ad choices" button appears only where consent applies. The app may also show Google Play's in-app review prompt, which Google Play handles under its own terms.
