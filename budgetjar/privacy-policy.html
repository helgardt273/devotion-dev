Budget Jar – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Budget Jar ("the app") is published by Devotion Dev. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

No account, no bank linking

Budget Jar does not ask you to sign up or log in. It never connects to your bank and never asks for your banking details.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. That includes your monthly income, jars (envelopes), budgets, transactions, notes, recurring items and settings (currency symbol and theme). We, the developer, never receive this data. It is not uploaded to any server.

Your phone's own Android backup (if you have turned it on) may include this app data in your Google account backup, under Google's terms.

Export and backup files

When you choose "Export transactions (CSV)" or "Back up everything (JSON)", the app writes a file to the location you pick in the Android file picker (for example your Downloads folder or Google Drive). When you choose "Restore from backup", the app reads only the file you pick. The app does not request any storage permission and cannot see your other files.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Budget Jar is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • app interaction and diagnostic information about ads (for example ad impressions and clicks).

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Consent (UMP)

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Ads > "Privacy & ad choices".

Optional rewarded video

In Settings you can choose to watch a video ad to turn off ads for 24 hours. This is optional. The app only stores the time when the ad-free period ends, on your device.

Permissions

The app itself declares only the Internet and network-state permissions, which are needed to load ads. All budgeting features work offline.

Children

Budget Jar is meant for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, permanently deletes all data the app has stored on your device. Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new "Last updated" date.

Other permissions and Google Play services

Google's ads SDK and Android libraries also add the advertising-ID, Android ad-services (Privacy Sandbox) and background-work permissions (such as wake lock and foreground service). The app does not show a prompt for these. The "Privacy & ad choices" button appears only where consent applies. The app may also show Google Play's in-app review prompt, which Google Play handles under its own terms.
