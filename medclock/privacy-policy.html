MedClock privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

MedClock ("the app") is a medication reminder published by Devotion Dev. This policy explains what information the app uses. In short: your medicines and history stay on your phone, there is no account, and apart from what Google's advertising service collects to show ads, information leaves your phone only through your own sharing, your own backup files or your own Android device backup.

Information stored on your phone

  • The medicines you add (name, strength, dose, schedule, dates, notes, stock count).
  • Optional photos of medicine boxes that you take with your camera or choose from your photos. They are kept in the app's private storage.
  • Your dose history (taken, skipped) and app settings (text size, colours, the name you type for reports).

This information is stored only on your device. We (the developer) never receive it. There is no account and no sign-up. If Android backup is switched on in your phone settings, Android may include the app's data in your own Google device backup.

Information you choose to share

"Share with family" creates a text or PDF report on your phone. It is only sent if you choose an app (for example WhatsApp or e-mail) and a person to send it to. "Save a backup file" writes a file to a place you choose. MedClock never sends anything by itself.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model, diagnostics and app usage related to ads. Google's use of this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Your medicine information is never shared with Google or any advertiser.

Where required by law (for example in the EU and UK), the app asks for your consent using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → "Privacy & ad choices". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show medicine reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): so reminders come at the exact minute.
  • Run at start-up: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Internet / network state: only for ads.

The app does not ask for camera, location, contacts, SMS, phone or storage permissions. Photos are taken with your phone's own camera app.

Children

MedClock is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Medical disclaimer

MedClock is a reminder tool. It does not give medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your doctor or pharmacist.

Deleting your data

Delete a medicine in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all its data from your phone.

Other permissions and Google Play services

Google's ads SDK and Android libraries also add the advertising-ID, Android ad-services (Privacy Sandbox) and background-work permissions (such as wake lock and foreground service). The app does not show a prompt for these. The "Privacy & ad choices" button appears only where consent applies. The app may also show Google Play's in-app review prompt, which Google Play handles under its own terms.
