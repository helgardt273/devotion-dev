TakeHome SA – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026. Publisher: Devotion Dev.

Summary

TakeHome SA is a salary and tax calculator. It has no account or sign-up. The amounts you enter and your settings stay on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses a device advertising identifier.

What the app stores on your device

  • The last values you entered in the calculator (salary, age band, retirement and medical aid amounts, bonus).
  • Your settings (theme, chosen tax year) and, if you used it, the time until which ads are switched off.

This information is kept in the app's private storage. We do not receive it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. If Android backup is turned on, it may be included in your own Google backup.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, diagnostic data and information about ad interactions to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Consent

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app shows Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time with "Privacy & ad choices" in Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

What we do not do

  • No account, no name, email or phone number is collected.
  • No location, contacts, photos or files are accessed.
  • Your salary details are never sent to us. They leave the app only if you tap Share and choose where to send them.

Permissions

Internet and network state – used only to load ads. All calculations work offline.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Other permissions and Google Play services

Google's ads SDK and Android libraries also add the advertising-ID, Android ad-services (Privacy Sandbox) and background-work permissions (such as wake lock and foreground service). The app does not show a prompt for these. The "Privacy & ad choices" button appears only where consent applies. The app may also show Google Play's in-app review prompt, which Google Play handles under its own terms.
