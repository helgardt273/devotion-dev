Mind Five privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Mind Five ("the app") is a set of daily puzzle games published by Devotion Dev. This policy explains what information the app handles.

No account, and your data stays on your phone

The app has no account or sign-up. Your game scores, daily workout results, streak, streak freezes, game levels and settings (theme, reminder time) are stored only on your device. If Android backup is switched on, Android may include them in your own Google device backup. We, the developer, do not receive, collect or store any of this information.

If you choose "Save backup" in Settings, the app writes a backup file to a location you pick using Android's file picker. That file is under your control. The app does not upload it anywhere.

You can delete all of this data at any time in Settings › Clear all progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications (Android 13 and newer). The reminder is scheduled on your phone, and no information leaves the device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service from Google LLC. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), and information about ad interactions and app performance (diagnostics). Google uses this information as described in its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Where required by law (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

Rewarded videos are always optional. They give a bonus streak freeze, a retry of a game, or 24 hours without ads.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Health

Mind Five is for fun and everyday practice. It is not a medical product and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Other permissions and Google Play services

Google's ads SDK and Android libraries also add the advertising-ID, Android ad-services (Privacy Sandbox) and background-work permissions (such as wake lock and foreground service). The app does not show a prompt for these. The "Privacy & ad choices" button appears only where consent applies. The app may also show Google Play's in-app review prompt, which Google Play handles under its own terms.
