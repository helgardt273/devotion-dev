Shift Roster – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Shift Roster ("the app") is published by Devotion Dev. This policy explains what information the app uses.

No account

The app has no account, sign-up or login. We do not run any server that receives your rosters.

What is stored on your device

Your rosters, shift types, one-off day changes (swaps, overtime, leave, sick days, public holidays), notes, reminder settings and app settings (theme, week start, public-holiday display) are stored only on your phone in the app's private storage. They are removed when you delete them in the app or uninstall it. Android's own device backup may include this data if you have backup turned on.

Files you export

When you export a calendar file (.ics), back up to a JSON file, or share a month picture, the file goes only where you choose (through the Android file picker or share sheet). The app does not upload it anywhere.

Notifications and alarms

If you turn on reminders or a wake-up alarm, the app schedules them on your device. Nothing is sent off the device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, general location derived from it, and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > "Privacy & ad choices". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: only for ads.
  • Notifications: for shift reminders, if you turn them on.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): optional, so the wake-up alarm rings on time.
  • Run at startup: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files (other than files you pick yourself).

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Other permissions and Google Play services

Google's ads SDK and Android libraries also add the advertising-ID, Android ad-services (Privacy Sandbox) and background-work permissions (such as wake lock and foreground service). The app does not show a prompt for these. The "Privacy & ad choices" button appears only where consent applies. The app may also show Google Play's in-app review prompt, which Google Play handles under its own terms.
