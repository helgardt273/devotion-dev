PDF Pocket privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

PDF Pocket is published by Devotion Dev.

Short version

PDF Pocket works fully offline. Your PDFs and pictures leave your phone only if you share them or save them to a place you choose. There is no account and no sign-up, and we run no servers. The only part of the app that uses the internet is Google AdMob, which shows ads.

Your files

When you open a PDF or pick photos, Android gives PDF Pocket access to just those files. All reading, merging, splitting, rotating, reordering, compressing and image-to-PDF conversion happens on your device. Results are saved only where you choose, or shared only with the app you choose. Temporary working copies are kept in the app's private cache and deleted automatically after a day. If you unlock a password-protected PDF, the password is used once on your phone and is not stored.

What is stored on your phone

  • A list of recently opened PDFs (file name, a link to the file, size, page count and the last page you read), so you can continue reading. You can clear it in Settings.
  • Your settings (theme and reading mode).
  • When an ad-free period you earned ends.

This information stays on the device. The recent-files list is not included in Android backups.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

PDF Pocket is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), your IP address, approximate location derived from it, diagnostic data, and information about how ads are shown and clicked, to serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In regions where the law requires it (for example the EU and UK), we ask for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time with "Privacy & ad choices" in Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Learn more in Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

PDF Pocket is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Permissions

INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE are used only for ads. PDF Pocket does not ask for storage, camera, location, contacts or phone permissions.

Other permissions and Google Play services

Google's ads SDK and Android libraries also add the advertising-ID, Android ad-services (Privacy Sandbox) and background-work permissions (such as wake lock and foreground service). The app does not show a prompt for these. The "Privacy & ad choices" button appears only where consent applies. The app may also show Google Play's in-app review prompt, which Google Play handles under its own terms.
