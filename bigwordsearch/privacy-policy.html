Big Word Search: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Big Word Search ("the app") is a word search game published by Devotion Dev. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

No account, and your game data stays on your device

You do not need an account to play, and the app never asks for your name, email address or any other personal details. Your puzzles in progress, solved-puzzle stats, Daily Puzzle streak and settings (such as letter size, theme, sound and vibration) are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete them at any time with Settings > Reset progress and stats, or by uninstalling the app. If you use Android's own device backup, Android may include this data in your personal Google backup.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this information under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. More about how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Ads are never shown while you are solving a puzzle. Optional rewarded videos (an extra hint, or 24 hours without ads) are shown only when you choose to watch them.

Your consent and choices

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings > Privacy & ad choices (this button appears where it applies). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android device settings.

Permissions

The app itself declares only the internet and network-state permissions, which the ads need. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. All puzzles work offline.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.

Other permissions and Google Play services

Google's ads SDK and Android libraries also add the advertising-ID, Android ad-services (Privacy Sandbox) and background-work permissions (such as wake lock and foreground service). The app does not show a prompt for these. The "Privacy & ad choices" button appears only where consent applies. The app may also show Google Play's in-app review prompt, which Google Play handles under its own terms.
