Trip Split privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Trip Split is published by Devotion Dev.

Summary

Trip Split is a fuel cost calculator, lift club ledger and fuel log. You don't need an account. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The app does not use your location. It shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your vehicles (name, fuel type, tank size)
  • Fill-ups (date, odometer reading, litres, price, total, full-tank flag, notes)
  • Fuel prices you save (coastal and inland, with dates)
  • Lift clubs (name, daily distance, costs, member first names and which days each person rode)
  • App preferences (units, currency symbol, theme)

This data is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We (the developer) have no server and never receive it. It may be included in your phone's own Android backup if you have that switched on. You can export a CSV file or a JSON backup yourself, and you choose where it is saved. When you share a trip cost or lift club summary, the text goes only to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp). Uninstalling the app deletes the data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under More > Privacy & ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Permissions

Trip Split itself declares only internet and network-state access, which Google AdMob needs to show ads. It does not ask for location, contacts, camera or storage permissions. Files are opened and saved through Android's own file picker.

Children

Trip Split is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Other permissions and Google Play services

Google's ads SDK and Android libraries also add the advertising-ID, Android ad-services (Privacy Sandbox) and background-work permissions (such as wake lock and foreground service). The app does not show a prompt for these. The "Privacy & ad choices" button appears only where consent applies. The app may also show Google Play's in-app review prompt, which Google Play handles under its own terms.
