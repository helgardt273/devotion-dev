Receipt Vault privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Receipt Vault ("the app") is published by Devotion Dev. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

No account, no server

The app has no sign-up and no account. We do not run a server, and we do not receive, collect or store your data.

What is stored on your device

Everything you enter stays on your phone, in the app's private storage: item names, stores, purchase dates, prices, categories, warranty details, return dates, serial numbers, notes, the photos you add (receipts, products, warranty cards) and your settings (theme, currency, reminder days). Photos are taken with your phone's own camera app or chosen with the system photo picker; the app does not have camera or storage permission.

Your data leaves the device only when you choose to: when you share a receipt, PDF or text with another app, when you export an inventory (CSV or PDF), or when you save a backup ZIP file to a location you pick. Android's own device backup may also copy the app's database and settings to your Google account if you have that turned on; photos are excluded from that cloud backup.

You can delete any item in the app, and uninstalling the app deletes all of its data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows reminders before a warranty or return window ends. Reminders are worked out on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), and information about ad interactions and app diagnostics, in order to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. This data is collected by Google, not by us. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are used. You can change your choice at any time with "Privacy & ad choices" in Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

In Settings you can watch an optional rewarded video to turn ads off for 24 hours.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: only to load ads.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and newer asks you): warranty and return reminders.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Other permissions and Google Play services

Google's ads SDK and Android libraries also add the advertising-ID, Android ad-services (Privacy Sandbox) and background-work permissions (such as wake lock and foreground service). The app does not show a prompt for these. The "Privacy & ad choices" button appears only where consent applies. The app may also show Google Play's in-app review prompt, which Google Play handles under its own terms.
