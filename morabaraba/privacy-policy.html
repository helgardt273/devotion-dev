Morabaraba: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Morabaraba ("the app") is published by Devotion Dev. This policy explains what data the app handles.

No account, data stays on your device

The app has no sign-up and no account. Your settings (theme, sound, vibration, rule options), your statistics (wins, losses, draws and streaks per level) and the game in progress are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We do not receive or upload this data. Android's own backup may include it in your Google account backup if you have device backup turned on. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), and information about the ads shown and your interaction with them, to deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time with "Privacy & ad choices" in Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Permissions

The app itself declares only the Internet and network-state permissions, which AdMob needs to load ads. The game itself works fully offline. The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Other permissions and Google Play services

Google's ads SDK and Android libraries also add the advertising-ID, Android ad-services (Privacy Sandbox) and background-work permissions (such as wake lock and foreground service). The app does not show a prompt for these. The "Privacy & ad choices" button appears only where consent applies. The app may also show Google Play's in-app review prompt, which Google Play handles under its own terms.
