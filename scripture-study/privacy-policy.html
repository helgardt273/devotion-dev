Scripture Study Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Scripture Study is published by Devotion Dev. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

The short version

We do not collect, store or share your personal information. The app has no ads, no account and no analytics.

Information you enter

Your journal entries, prayers, verse notes, highlights, reading progress and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us, and we cannot see them.

Internet use

The app does not connect to the internet itself. If you tap the Listen button in the Bible reader, your web browser opens bible.com, a website we do not run. That website's own privacy policy applies.

Sharing

If you choose to share a verse, Android lets you pick the app to share it with. The text goes only where you send it, and that app's own privacy policy applies.

Android backup

If device backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may include the app's data in your personal backup, under the settings of your Google account. We cannot access that backup. You can turn it off in your device settings.

Deleting your information

You can delete individual journal entries and prayers in the app. To delete everything, clear the app's storage in your device settings or uninstall the app. Any copy in your own Android backup is controlled through your Google account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

For questions about this policy, email devotiondev022@gmail.com.
