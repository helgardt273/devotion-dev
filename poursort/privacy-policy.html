Pour & Sort: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Pour & Sort ("the app") is a puzzle game published by Devotion Dev. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

No account, and your game data stays on your device

The app has no account or sign-up. Your level progress, stars, statistics, Daily Puzzle streak, settings and saved games are stored only on your device. We do not receive, collect or sell this data. Android's own backup may include it in your personal Google device backup, if you have backup turned on. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID,
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived,
  • information about ad interactions, and diagnostic and performance data.

This data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Google settings.

Consent

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time with "Privacy & ad choices" in the app's Settings.

Optional rewarded videos

Some helpers (extra undos, an extra jar, a hint, or 24 hours without ads) can be earned by choosing to watch a video ad. Watching is always optional.

Permissions

The app itself declares only internet and network-state access, which are used to load ads. It does not access your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or location.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Other permissions and Google Play services

Google's ads SDK and Android libraries also add the advertising-ID, Android ad-services (Privacy Sandbox) and background-work permissions (such as wake lock and foreground service). The app does not show a prompt for these. The "Privacy & ad choices" button appears only where consent applies. The app may also show Google Play's in-app review prompt, which Google Play handles under its own terms.
