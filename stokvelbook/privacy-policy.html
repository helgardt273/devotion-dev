Stokvel Book privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Stokvel Book ("the app") is published by Devotion Dev. It is a record book for stokvels, savings clubs, burial societies and similar groups. It does not move, hold or transfer money.

No account, data stays on your phone

The app has no sign-up and no account. Everything you enter (groups, member names and phone numbers, payments, fines, payouts, meeting minutes, settings and a hashed PIN if you turn on the PIN lock) is stored only on your device. If Android backup is switched on, Android may include this data in your own Google device backup. We do not receive, see or store any of it.

When you pick a phone number from your contacts, Android's own contact picker gives the app only the single contact you choose. The app does not read your contact list and does not ask for the contacts permission.

Backups, exports and sharing

Backups (JSON), CSV exports and shared statements (text or PDF) are created only when you ask, and saved or sent to the place you choose (for example a folder, Google Drive or WhatsApp). Those services handle the files under their own privacy policies.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from it, and app interaction and diagnostic data to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), and you can change your choice at any time from "Privacy & ad choices" in Settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Delete groups inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Other permissions and Google Play services

Google's ads SDK and Android libraries also add the advertising-ID, Android ad-services (Privacy Sandbox) and background-work permissions (such as wake lock and foreground service). The app does not show a prompt for these. The "Privacy & ad choices" button appears only where consent applies. The app may also show Google Play's in-app review prompt, which Google Play handles under its own terms.
