DueDay privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

DueDay ("the app") is a bill, debit order and subscription tracker published by Devotion Dev. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

No account, no bank connection

DueDay does not need an account or sign-up and never connects to your bank. It does not make payments.

What the app stores, and where

The bills you add (names, amounts, currencies, categories, payment methods, dates, notes, colours and icons), the payments you mark as paid or skipped, and your settings (pay day, theme, main currency, reminder time) are stored only on your device. We do not receive, see or keep a copy of this information.

If you export a CSV file or save a backup, the file is written to the location you choose with Android's file picker. Where it goes from there is up to you. Android's own device backup may include the app's data if you have it switched on in your phone's settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, DueDay shows reminders before bills are due and before free trials end. Reminders are created on the device; nothing is sent to a server.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

DueDay is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process your device's advertising ID, your approximate location derived from your IP address, information about how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic information. Google uses this to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Consent

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), DueDay asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > "Privacy & ad choices". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: needed only to load ads. All features work offline.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later): for bill and free-trial reminders. You can refuse.

DueDay does not ask for location, contacts, SMS, phone, camera or storage permissions.

Children

DueDay is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete bills inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Other permissions and Google Play services

Google's ads SDK and Android libraries also add the advertising-ID, Android ad-services (Privacy Sandbox) and background-work permissions (such as wake lock and foreground service). The app does not show a prompt for these. The "Privacy & ad choices" button appears only where consent applies. The app may also show Google Play's in-app review prompt, which Google Play handles under its own terms.
