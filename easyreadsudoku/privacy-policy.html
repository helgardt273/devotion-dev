Privacy Policy for Easy-Read Sudoku

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Easy-Read Sudoku ("the app") is published by Devotion Dev. This policy explains what information the app handles.

No account, data stays on your device

The app has no account and no sign-up. Your games in progress, settings, statistics (games won, best and average times) and your Daily Sudoku streak are stored only on your device. The app does not send this information to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. If you use Android's own device backup, Android may include this data in your personal Google backup.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads are shown on menu screens and after a finished puzzle, never while you are solving. Optional rewarded videos can give you extra hints or 24 hours without ads.

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud and abuse prevention. See Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent (UMP)

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time under Settings > "Privacy & ad choices". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Permissions

The app itself declares only internet and network-state access, which are needed to load ads. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone or files.

Children

The app is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.

Other permissions and Google Play services

Google's ads SDK and Android libraries also add the advertising-ID, Android ad-services (Privacy Sandbox) and background-work permissions (such as wake lock and foreground service). The app does not show a prompt for these. The "Privacy & ad choices" button appears only where consent applies. The app may also show Google Play's in-app review prompt, which Google Play handles under its own terms.
